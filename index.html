Not a medical device. This is a research and portfolio project. It is not cleared by ANVISA, the FDA, or any regulator, and nothing it produces should be used to make a decision about a patient. Every output the system generates carries a human-review flag by design — a radiologist reads it, and the radiologist signs it. All data used here is from public research datasets. No patient data from clinical practice enters this project.
